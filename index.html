Counselling and therapy

Change and uncertainty

Patterns and habits

How it works

We meet, talk, and work things through at your pace

01

We begin with what feels present

There is no expectation to open up straight away or explain everything perfectly.

02

We make sense of what has been building

Over time, we can look at patterns, pressures, and the way you respond to them.

03

We find steadier ways forward

The aim is not to force change, but to help things feel more manageable and clear.
